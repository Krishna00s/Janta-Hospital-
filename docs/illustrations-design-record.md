# Janta Hospital — Medical Illustration Asset & Design Record

## 1. Design System & Aesthetic Overview
To transition temporarily away from photography-heavy sections (while strictly preserving the authentic hospital exterior photograph in the homepage hero), Janta Hospital adopts a unified, clean, static medical illustration language.

### Core Visual Principles
- **Simplified, faceless medical professionals**: Clean silhouettes without facial features (no eyes, nose, or mouth), representing caring clinical staff objectively without fabricating likenesses of specific individuals.
- **Flat vector aesthetic**: Clean lines, subtle planar shadows, no realistic skin textures, 3D cartoons, or animated elements.
- **Strict Brand Alignment**: Matches Janta Hospital's mint-and-sage palette:
  - Deep Medical Green: `#0A5C4F`
  - Deep Emergency Teal: `#0D7A68`
  - Emerald Primary: `#149E86`
  - Accent Green: `#75BCA5`
  - Medium Sage: `#A9DCCB`
  - Light Mint: `#E0F3EC` / `#EBF7F2`
  - Clean Medical Coat: `#FFFFFF` with `#E2EDE8` / `#D5E6DF` seams
  - Natural Flat Skin Silhouettes: `#F0C7A8` / `#E5B896` / `#E0B18E`
